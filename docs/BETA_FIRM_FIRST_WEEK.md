# Private beta: the first-week note for a firm

**Nothing in this file has been sent.** Send it yourself, a day or two after the firm has signed up (or
paste it into the invitation if you prefer one email), with your own words added. The invitation itself
comes from Admin -> Beta waitlist -> **Send invitation email**.

Placeholders: `{{first_name}}`, `{{firm_name}}`, `{{your_name}}`.

---

> Subject: Your first week with Finverlo
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Thanks for trying Finverlo with {{firm_name}}. Here is what a good first week looks like, so you
> know what to do and what to expect from me. It is early software, so please keep your existing
> records alongside it for the whole beta. Checking that Finverlo's figures match yours is the most
> useful thing you can do.
>
> **Day 1: set up (about 30 minutes)**
> - Sign in and set up your firm, then add one client: your own books, or one client who has agreed.
> - Import one month of their expenses, customers or products from the spreadsheet you already have
>   (Data Upload in the menu; a small file is fine).
> - Open the Overview and compare the totals with your own figures.
>
> **Days 2 to 4: use it for real, on a small scale**
> - Scan a supplier bill (Purchases -> Scan a bill) and check the items it reads. Edit them if needed.
> - Ask the AI Assistant a question you would normally answer by hand, such as "what did we spend on
>   supplies this month?", and see whether it agrees with you.
> - Invite a colleague (they sign up first, then you add them from the Staff page).
> - If it goes well, add a second client.
>
> **Day 5: a short call (15 minutes)**
> - I will ask three things: did the totals match, what took longer than it should, and what do you
>   still do outside Finverlo.
>
> **Please do not**
> - Upload several clients' real records yet, or send bank logins, statements or tax IDs by email.
>   Start with one client who is happy to be involved.
>
> **If something looks wrong**
> - Tell me straight away, even if it is small, with the page you were on and what you expected. Any
>   number that does not match your own is the top priority. Write to support@finverlo.com and I will
>   reply personally.
>
> It is free during the beta and no payment details are involved. Thank you for helping me get this
> right.
>
> {{your_name}}

## What to do on your side

- Send it a day or two after sign-up, when they have had time to look around.
- Put the call in the diary when you send it, so "Day 5" is a real date.
- Keep a short note for each firm: totals that matched or not, where they got stuck, what they still do
  elsewhere. That is the whole point of the beta.
