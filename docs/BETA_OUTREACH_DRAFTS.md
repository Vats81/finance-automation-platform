# Private beta - outreach drafts

**Nothing in this file has been sent, and nothing will be sent by the app.**
These are drafts for the owner to edit and send personally, to people the owner
has chosen, when the owner decides. Do not send until you have identified the
recipients and explicitly approved the wording.

Positioning: Finverlo is a **bookkeeping and reporting assistant** for small
businesses that keep their books in spreadsheets today. It is a free, private,
supervised beta - not a finished accounting system, and not a replacement for an
accountant. Be upfront about that in every message.

Placeholders: `{{first_name}}`, `{{their_business}}`, `{{your_name}}`,
`{{support_email}}`, `{{beta_link}}` (the unlinked `/beta` interest form).

---

## 1. Short note to someone you know personally

> Subject: Would you try a bookkeeping tool I'm building? (free, small beta)
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> I'm building a small tool that turns the spreadsheets small businesses already
> keep into clear monthly sales, expenses and profit reports. It's early, so I'm
> opening a free, hands-on beta for just a few businesses, and {{their_business}}
> came to mind.
>
> If you're interested, add your details here: {{beta_link}}. That only
> registers interest - it doesn't create an account, and I'll reach out
> personally if there's a place. Please don't send bank details or real
> financial files; we'll set things up together later, with sample data first.
>
> If you'd rather not, no problem at all.
>
> {{your_name}}
